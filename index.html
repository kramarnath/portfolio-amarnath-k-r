About Me

Who I Am

I started with an ITI in Electronics Mechanic, spent time in industry, then completed a Diploma in Electronics Engineering and a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from GEC Thrissur. At FCI-OEN Connectors I inspected and tested connector assemblies and operated production machinery. At ERCMPU Ltd I diagnosed and fixed faults in industrial control equipment and helped keep production running. Those years taught me to pay attention to detail, fix problems methodically and understand how hardware testing works in practice.

In college and through my projects I gained hands-on experience with ESP32, STM32, UART, SPI, I2C, GPIO, sensors, Python and Embedded C, and with debugging real hardware. I have designed my own PCBs, written firmware for embedded systems, worked with different interfaces, and designed a 2.4 GHz RF front-end transmitter in Cadence Virtuoso.

I enjoy hardware testing, embedded systems, test automation, debugging and working with lab instruments. As someone new to the field, I want to learn how teams build test methods, instruments and fixtures, and to contribute my hardware and embedded skills along the way. I am looking for embedded, IoT and core electronics roles across Kerala and South India.
