About Me

Who Am I

My journey in electronics began with an ITI, followed by a Diploma and a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Government Engineering College, Thrissur (GEC Thrissur). This progression gave me opportunities to understand electronics from both practical and theoretical perspectives.

I've worked on various projects involving microcontrollers, circuit design, PCB development, and hardware interfacing. I've also explored different electronics tools, simulation environments, and CAD softwares through the journey, along with programming in Embedded C and Python.

I enjoy building things, experimenting with new technologies, understanding how hardware works, and solving practical engineering problems. I'm always curious to explore new areas and turn ideas into working projects.

Beyond academics, I've gained industrial experience in manufacturing, quality inspection, equipment testing, and troubleshooting industrial control systems.

There's more to my journey than a few paragraphs. Feel free to explore my projects, experience, and technical interests to know more about me.
